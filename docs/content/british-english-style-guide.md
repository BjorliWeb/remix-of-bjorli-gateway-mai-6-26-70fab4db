# British English style guide – bjorli.no

Applies to all English (`/en/`) content: dictionaries (`src/i18n/locales/en.ts`, `src/i18n/legacyTranslations.ts`), page copy objects (`en:` blocks in `src/pages/*`), CMS fixtures and data (`src/lib/cms/*`), SEO titles and leads (`src/lib/seo/routeSeo.ts`, `src/lib/seo/routeLeads.ts`) and alt text.

Primary audience: British families planning a ski holiday in Norway, plus couples and friends who want skiing, nature and a relaxed stay in the mountains. Many are first-time visitors who don't know Bjorli, Romsdalen or the Rauma Line.

Other languages are not covered here and must not be changed when editing English.

## Editorial position

Bjorli is a Norwegian mountain destination for ski holidays, time together and things to do through the year. In winter the focus is skiing, cabin life and the mountains. The Rauma Line is part of what makes Bjorli distinctive.

- Give Bjorli a clear identity, describe the holiday concretely and connect each activity to what the guest gets out of it.
- Combine inspiration with practical planning, and make the next step obvious.
- Describe what exists today. Mention future projects only where they already appear, and clearly as plans.
- Keep **Bjorli** (the destination) separate from **Bjorli Ski Resort** (the ski area run by Bjorli Skisenter).

## Tone

Warm, welcoming, concrete, credible, relaxed and confident. Easy to follow for a first-time visitor.

Do:
- Use "you" and "your" where it makes the text more direct.
- Use natural contractions: you'll, there's, you're, it's.
- Keep paragraphs short, usually 1–3 sentences.
- Prefer active verbs and concrete descriptions.
- Use sentence case for headings and buttons ("Find your accommodation", not "Find Your Accommodation").
- Make button labels say what happens on click.

Avoid:
- Literal translations from Norwegian: "real mountain days", "nature experiences", "the natural gathering point of the destination", "calm mountain basecamp".
- Repeated superlatives and vague words: ultimate, world-class, magical, unforgettable, hidden gem, breathtaking.
- leverage, unlock, seamless, elevate, nestled.
- Heavy British slang or a forced matey tone.
- Em dashes. Use a full stop, a comma, a colon or brackets. En dashes are fine in ranges (10:00–16:00, 4–20 September).
- Repeating "family-friendly" or "snow-sure" in every paragraph.

## Facts

Existing marketing copy is a starting point, not proof. Follow the project's fact rule (`CLAUDE.md`, "Faktaregel"): every published factual claim must be traceable to an authoritative source or owner confirmation.

Do not introduce or strengthen claims about:
- guaranteed snow or specific opening dates
- "Norway's best" or "most snow-sure" resort, or being "first to open"
- short or no lift queues, or few people
- ski-in/ski-out, walking distances, or getting around without a car
- English-speaking instructors
- direct flights, routes, transport services or journey times
- low prices, included equipment or packages
- restaurants, hotels or activities being open all season
- terrain or facilities "for all levels"

"Snow-sure" may be used sparingly where the project already uses it as positioning. It must never read as a guarantee. If in doubt, prefer a precise, less categorical phrase and list the question in the delivery report.

Confirmed facts in use (see also `CLAUDE.md`):
- Children aged 0 to 6 ski free and don't need their own Keycard.
- Ålesund Airport (Vigra) to Bjorli: around 2½ hours by car (Rome2rio, checked 15 October 2026). Never "2 hours".
- No direct London Gatwick–Ålesund flights were listed for winter 2026/27 (Norwegian fare calendar, checked 15 October 2026).

## Formats

- Dates: 15 October 2026; 4–20 September; November–May (check the season first).
- Times: 24-hour clock, 10:00–16:00.
- Numbers: 1,225 metres; 170 km.
- Prices stay in NOK in the project's existing format. Don't add currency conversions.
- Spelling: -ise (organise), -our (colour), centre, metre, kilometre, travelling, cosy, programme, licence (noun) / license (verb), sledging.

## Navigation labels (EN)

Desktop menu: Winter · Summer · Bjorli Ski Resort · Weather & webcams · Stay · Things to do · What’s on · Inspiration · Travel, plus the seasonal button (Buy lift passes in winter, Where to stay in summer).

"Stay" and "Travel" are deliberate short forms: with "Where to stay" and "Getting here" the menu wrapped onto two lines at 1280 px (measured 15 October 2026). Page headings, footer and links use the full terms (Where to stay, Getting here, Food & drink, Contact, Lift passes, Opening times, Ski hire, Ski school).

## Titles and search themes

- Format: `Topic | Qualifier`. Avoid a double brand name (no "Bjorli … | Bjorli").
- Fixed titles (brief, 15 October 2026): homepage "Bjorli | Family Ski Holidays in Norway"; winter "Winter Holidays in Norway | Discover Bjorli"; ski resort "Bjorli Ski Resort | Skiing in Norway"; ski hire "Ski Hire in Bjorli | Skis & Snowboards"; accommodation "Where to Stay in Bjorli | Cabins & Apartments". Other titles use sentence case.
- Working targets: about 50–60 characters for titles and 140–160 for descriptions. These are guides, not rules.
- Each page has one job. The homepage is the entry point, `/en/winter/` covers winter holidays in Norway, `/en/ski-holiday-norway/` is the UK planning guide and `/en/family/` covers families across both seasons.
- "Gateway to winter" is used only on the winter page. It is an editorial line, and its search volume has not been verified.
- Use "snow-sure", "Easter ski holidays" and "ski cabins in Norway" only when the facts back them up.
- Keep the H1 in the first HTML (`src/lib/seo/routeLeads.ts`) identical to the H1 on the hydrated page.
- Search Console baseline: `docs/content/uk-search-console-baseline-2026-10-15.md`. Page matrix: `docs/content/en-page-matrix.md`.

## Technical notes
